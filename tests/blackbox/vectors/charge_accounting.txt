# A 100 A (10000 cA) load on the default 20 mV/A sensor fits within both the
# 12-bit 3.3 V (165 A full scale) and 10-bit 3.0 V (150 A full scale) ADC
# models. After the 50-sample current-filter startup, one second consumes
# about 27 mAh (100000 mA / 3600).
# 50 ms telemetry slots align the last packet with each assertion below.
@tuning am32

@config
eeprom.telemetry_on_interval=21
actual_current=10000

@sequence
ticks 1s | throttle=0 | control_ticks>=20000 actual_current=10000 consumed_mah>=27 consumed_mah<=28 kiss_consumed_mah>=27 kiss_consumed_mah<=28
ticks 1s | throttle=0 | control_ticks>=40000 actual_current=10000 consumed_mah>=54 consumed_mah<=56 kiss_consumed_mah>=54 kiss_consumed_mah<=56
